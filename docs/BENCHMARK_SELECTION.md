# Benchmark selection

The requested [AI4Bharat IndicQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/IndicQA) is retained as the primary Hindi/Tamil evaluation source. Its [official loader](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/IndicQA/blob/main/IndicQA.py) declares 11 language configurations and only a **test** split. It is CC BY 4.0. No IndicQA test record is repurposed for training.

The named [Indic-QA paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13522) points to [a different author repository](https://github.com/ayushayush591/IndicQA-Benchmark) and [dataset release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ayushayush591/IndicQA_Benchmark). The broader paper benchmark incorporates AI4Bharat IndicQA alongside translated and synthetic QA resources. The releases are not interchangeable. This implementation evaluates the explicitly named AI4Bharat component; it does not claim full reproduction of every task in the paper.

No reliable English parallel-ID mapping is established for this component. [SQuAD v1.1](https://rajpurkar.github.io/SQuAD-explorer/) is therefore the separate English benchmark permitted by the brief. Its official development set becomes this project's sealed English evaluation/test population. Source training articles are grouped to create a separate validation set. SQuAD's [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkar/squad) declares CC BY-SA 4.0; attribution and share-alike obligations remain applicable to redistributed data.

The choice supports context-grounded QA, Unicode/scripts, source provenance and explicit unanswerable IndicQA examples. It does not by itself establish campus-domain transfer, parallel cross-language difficulty, reliable translated-policy semantics or performance gains.

## Approved experimental-design extension

The original specification assumes an IndicQA training split that does not exist. The user explicitly approved adding the published [L3Cube IndicSQuAD](https://huggingface.co/datasets/l3cube-pune/indic-squad) Hindi/Tamil training splits. Its [paper and author release](https://aclanthology.org/2025.paclic-1.70/) describe translated SQuAD data. This is a separate training source, not a replacement for IndicQA evaluation. The publisher declares CC BY 4.0; original SQuAD attribution and share-alike obligations are retained. Machine translations are not claimed independently human-verified.

Only records whose preserved question IDs map to original SQuAD v1.1 English training IDs are eligible. Source IDs absent from that training set, empty translations, script mismatches and answer/context mismatches are excluded. A stable hash with seed 42 selects up to 10,000 eligible rows per target language before document grouping. Canonical English source-document and question groups connect the translated training versions, preventing translated copies from crossing train/validation. Published supplemental dev/test splits are not used, and no English parallel mapping to IndicQA evaluation is claimed. See the generated supplemental report for actual counts and checksums. A further explicit importer remains available for future separately registered studies.

Possible supplemental sources need source-specific license and provenance review. For example, [AI4Bharat Indic-Rag-Suite](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Indic-Rag-Suite) publishes synthetic QA training material, but its generated-data provenance and Wikipedia-derived content require an explicit extension to this study. It was inspected, not downloaded or substituted. An unlabeled-license Tamil SQuAD upload was also not adopted. No extra corpus is silently treated as the requested benchmark.
